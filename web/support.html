01 — Join the community

Participation is the first way to support.

A token only goes as far as the people around it. Taking part, asking good questions and sharing accurate information all help the project grow in the open.

Take part, not just hold

Read what the project publishes, question what is unclear, and test what goes live. Every page on this site can be read without a wallet, so taking part never depends on owning anything.

Read the publishing principles

Help grow awareness

Share official information with people who are curious about Solana. Link to this website rather than screenshots or third-party summaries, so the details people read stay accurate.

See the official token record

Keep the record clear

Clarity is part of the contribution: wording that is easy to misread is worth correcting. Changes are published where the original statement lives, so the project keeps one consistent record.

02 — Build with us

Bring the skills you already have.

UnitedSOLANA is a small, transparent project. Developers, designers, creators and innovators who care about clarity and safety are the people who move it forward.

Developers & designers

The website is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step and no framework. A small, readable codebase is an easy place to start — interfaces, accessibility and documentation all matter as much as new features.

Read the project information

Creators & educators

Writers, educators and makers can turn technical detail into material people actually understand. Explainers, visuals and well-sourced walkthroughs help newcomers find their footing on Solana.

Review the principles

Propose before you build

Larger ideas — new tools, integrations or product directions — are reviewed as proposals first. Anything that would sign, transfer or move funds needs a separate technical and security review before it ships.

Read the transparency standards

03 — Support ecosystem development

Help build what comes next.

Transparent opportunities

If a funding or support opportunity opens, it will be published here with its purpose, what it covers and how it is accounted for. Nothing is collected through this website today, and this page carries no payment or transaction functionality.

How this page grows

Built to expand, one confirmed step at a time.

Each area above is a chapter with its own link and a grid of cards. When a new support initiative, community programme, ecosystem contribution, partnership or participation opportunity is approved, it becomes another card inside the relevant chapter — or a new chapter following the same pattern. No section needs to be rebuilt to make room for it.

Nothing is added until its details can be verified and published in the project's own words, so this page grows without ever promising more than the project can show.

What this page does not do

  • It publishes no donation address or payment route.
  • It offers no investment, yield or financial return.
  • It processes no payments, transfers or transactions.
  • It claims no partnership or endorsement that is unconfirmed.
